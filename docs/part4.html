An honest test

The forest scores well when the test minutes are drawn at random. What happens when it must predict a point it has never seen?
Minutes of one visit are near copies of each other. In a random split the test minute has its neighbors in the training set, so the model partly remembers instead of predicting [1].

R2=1−∑i​(yi​−yˉ​)2∑i​(yi​−y^​i​)2​

yi measured PM2.5 of minute i (µg/m³) · ŷi prediction for that minute, made by a model that did not see it · ȳ mean of all minutes. R² = 1 is perfect, R² = 0 is no better than always guessing the mean. The same forest drops by about 41 % when whole points are held out.

Takeaway. Ask what the model will face in use. For a new spot on a campus, the honest test leaves whole points out. Many rows are not many independent observations: This dataset has 468 minutes but only 18 measurement points.

What does it rely on?

Which input will matter most for the forest's PM2.5 predictions on a campus it has not seen?
Traffic first, the background of the day a close second, people at the door third. Humidity hardly matters, because Part 1 already removed its effect on the sensor.
Traffic comes first, the background of the day a close second, people at the door third. Humidity hardly matters, because Part 1 already removed its effect on the sensor.

Permutation importance: The drop of R² when one input is shuffled. The forest is trained on two campuses and tested on the third, and the three results are averaged.

Ij​=R2(X)−R2(X with column j shuffled)

Ij importance of input j · shuffling breaks the link between that input and PM2.5 while keeping its values. A large drop means the model needs the input; a value near zero means it does not [2].

In everyday life: To test whether a team needs its goalkeeper, swap the goalkeeper for a random player and watch the score. The bigger the drop, the more the team relied on that player.

Importance describes the model, not the world. In this synthetic dataset the forest recovers the rules that generated the data. With real NOKTA data an importance is a hypothesis to check against the field forms.
