NOKTA Lab · 1:55 to 2:00 and after the session

The bench

The NOKTA Lab gathers the three 3D tools of the session on one page. In the last minutes of the workshop it serves for questions from the audience. On the analysis days of 21 to 24 November it lets the teams return to the campus twin, the cloud of minutes and the model surface. The fourth tab, the debrief, turns what was done in this browser during the session into a short field report.

resultsTwo-round means

This is the campus twin of Part 1, now for all three campuses. The menus choose a campus, a single round or the mean of both rounds, and a channel. Play walks the station through the round again, while Results shows the final columns at once. A click on a point fills the point card on the right with its values.

Point card

Click one of the six points in the scene to see its values in both rounds.

The cloud shows the clean minutes as in Part 3. The menu k chooses the number of groups, and the silhouette and J values above show how well the groups are separated. The groups are those that scikit-learn found in the Colab notebook, numbered from the coolest to the warmest center. The button Point types colors the same minutes by the place where they were measured, so the two views can be compared.

PM2.5 over traffic and background

The surface shows the PM2.5 that the forest of Part 4 predicts for every combination of traffic and background PM2.5. All other inputs keep the values of the sliders on the right. Higher and darker parts mean higher PM2.5, and the number above the sliders gives the prediction for the current scenario.

predicted PM2.5 (µg/m³)

Everything shown here comes from this browser and is never sent anywhere. In a live session, the debrief shown at the close is the one produced by the presenter's browser, which records the answers that the presenter entered for the room.

Next steps for the NOKTA teams. From 21 to 24 November the teams run the Colab notebook on their own NOKTA files. In Section 0, one setting switches the notebook from the synthetic data to the uploaded files. On 28 November each team brings one solution card per thematic table to the workshop. For the congress it brings one figure whose caption states the averaging time of its values.
Colab

Section 0 of the Colab notebook explains how real NOKTA files are loaded instead of the synthetic data. It also shows how the column names of another logger are matched to the NOKTA names.
